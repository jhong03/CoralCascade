# Privacy Policy — Coral Cascade

**Last updated: 20 July 2026**

Coral Cascade does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## What stays on your device

Your level progress, best scores, stars, pearls and reef decorations are saved only on
your device, using the operating system's standard app storage. Nothing is uploaded
anywhere, and the developer cannot see it.

## No accounts

The game has no sign-in. It never asks for your name, email address, or any other
identifying information.

## No network, no advertising, no analytics

This version of the game does not connect to the internet, does not display adverts, and
does not use analytics or tracking of any kind. No advertising identifier is read or
transmitted.

## Children

Because the game collects no data at all, it collects none from children either.

## Deleting your data

**Settings → Reset ALL progress** erases everything the game has saved. Uninstalling the
app removes it as well.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds adverts or analytics, this policy and the Google Play Data safety
information will both be updated before that version is released.

## Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the developer contact address shown on the Coral
Cascade listing on Google Play.
