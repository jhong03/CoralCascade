### Copy notes

- The character counts above are **measured, not estimated** (my first pass
  guessed and was wrong by 160 on the long one). Re-measure after any edit:
  ```powershell
  $md = [IO.File]::ReadAllText("store\STORE_LISTING.md")
  [regex]::Matches($md, '(?s)```\r?\n(.*?)```') | ForEach-Object {
    ($_.Groups[1].Value -replace "`r`n","`n").TrimEnd("`n").Length }
  ```
- Scoring claims are checked against `Scoring.cs`: matched 10, dropped **20 (2×)**,
  chain-knocked **40 (4×)**. Wave names and counts come from `LevelCatalog.Specs`
  (14 waves × 5 = 70).
- Hyphens, not em-dashes or "·" — the Console's text fields have mangled non-ASCII
  punctuation before, and it is not worth the risk for a typographic nicety.
- No "#1", "best", "top", testimonials or "Download now" — all of these are
  disallowed or penalised in Play listings.

### ⚠️ Claims audit — what breaks when ads land

This copy was written so **nothing in it becomes false** when
`ADS_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` is executed. Keep it that way:

- **Do NOT add "no ads", "ad-free", or "no in-app purchases".** All three become
  false the day AdMob lands, and a stale claim in a live listing is a policy
  problem, not just an embarrassment.
- **Do NOT add "plays offline"** for the same reason — it is true of the game
  today, but it invites a complaint once rewarded ads exist.
- "No lives / no energy / no timers" is safe: it stays true under the planned
  monetization, because pearls are cosmetic-only and rewarded ads are opt-in.

## 2. Graphics

| Asset | File | Spec | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Store icon | `store/icon_512.png` | 512×512, **32-bit PNG with alpha**, ≤1024 KB | ✅ 512×512, 32bpp ARGB, 81 KB |
| Feature graphic | `store/feature_graphic.png` | 1024×500, **24-bit PNG, no alpha** (or JPEG) | ✅ 1024×500, 24bpp RGB, 174 KB |
| Phone screenshots | `store/screenshots/` | 2–8 required; see §3 | ⬜ you capture these |

Both graphics are generated by `store/render_feature.ps1` (re-runnable). It
replicates `PrimitiveSprites.GlossyOrb` / `OrbGloss` pixel-for-pixel — including
the fact that Unity's `Mathf.SmoothStep(from, to, t)` maps *into* `[from,to]`, so
the orb's "rim shadow" dims the whole ball rather than just its edge. Get that
wrong and the store art is visibly brighter than the game.

**The alpha rule is not cosmetic.** A 32-bit feature graphic is rejected on
upload. The script flattens onto an opaque 24bpp surface for exactly this reason.
The *icon* is the opposite — it must keep its alpha channel.

## 3. Screenshots — capture these while you play-test

Play needs **2 minimum, 8 maximum**. Four or more at 1080px+ is the bar for
promotional consideration, so aim for six.

### The shot list, best first

1. **Mid-cascade.** Debris falling, a praise banner up (`NICE CASCADE!` /
   `CHAIN REACTION!`), score popups in flight. This is the one that sells the
   game — it shows the thing no other bubble shooter screenshot shows.
2. **A busy board** with the star meter partly filled and the shots chip visible.
3. **A mechanic reef** — ice and stone together reads best (Glacier Vault, 56–60).
4. **The rising tide** — danger line pulsing, tide chip flipped to urgent coral.
5. **My Reef** — a stocked aquarium, ideally with a rare fish in frame.
6. **The Adventure map** — a run of gold stars on cleared nodes shows progression.

Optional 7th: the win overlay with three stars and the pearl payout.

### Capturing and cropping

Android: **Power + Volume Down**. Then run:

```powershell
store\crop_screenshots.ps1
```

It reads every PNG/JPG in `store/screenshots/raw/` and writes compliant copies to
`store/screenshots/`.

**Why cropping is mandatory, not optional:** Play requires the longest side to be
no more than **twice** the shortest. A modern phone screenshot is around
1080×2340 — that is 2.17×, so **your raw screenshots will be rejected as-is.**
The script trims to at most 2:1 (1080×2160), taking the excess evenly off the top
and bottom, which on most phones removes precisely the status bar and the gesture
bar. That is a bonus: Google asks that screenshots not include added chrome.

It also flattens to 24-bit (screenshots may not carry an alpha channel) and
fails loudly if a shot falls outside the 320–3840 px bounds.

Do not add device frames, marketing text, or background art around the
screenshots — Google's guidance specifically excludes all three, and taglines
must not exceed 20% of the image.

## 4. What still blocks the listing

These are Console-side, not asset-side. Full detail in `PLAY_STORE_CHECKLIST.md`.

- [ ] Keystore + Play App Signing (needs your password — I can't create it)
- [ ] Hosted privacy-policy URL (the in-app text exists; it needs a public page)
- [ ] Data safety form — "no data collected" is honest **today only**
- [ ] Content rating (IARC) questionnaire
- [ ] Target audience declaration — the highest-leverage answer on the whole
      submission. A bright cartoon fish game declaring 13+ still gets a second
      look; declaring children pulls in the full Families policy, and the ads
      plan already assumes AdMob self-certification rather than mediation.
- [ ] App access declaration ("all functionality available without special access")
- [ ] Closed testing: 12 testers × 14 continuous days for a personal account
